Apache-2.0, with attribution

A permissive license with an express patent grant. Proprietary use is allowed, but it is not obligation-free.

Short version. You may use, modify and distribute ReproParcel, including inside proprietary applications, if you keep the copyright, license and NOTICE attribution, include the license, mark modified files and respect the patent and trademark terms. There is no copyleft and no network source-offer requirement.

What the license gives you

  • The right to use, reproduce, modify, distribute and sublicense the work.
  • The right to combine it with proprietary code and distribute the combined application.
  • An express patent grant from contributors, subject to the license's termination conditions.
  • No requirement to publish your own source code.

What you must do

  • Retain notices. Keep copyright, patent, trademark and attribution notices from the source.
  • Include the license. Ship a copy of the Apache License 2.0 with any distribution.
  • Preserve NOTICE. Include the relevant NOTICE attribution in distributions.
  • Mark modifications. State clearly in modified files that you changed them.
  • Respect the trademark terms. Do not imply endorsement.

What it does not grant

  • No trademark or brand license. The ReproParcel name and mark may be used only for accurate, non-endorsing references.
  • No warranty. The software is provided “as is”.
  • No immunity from third-party patents beyond the express grant.
  • No sublicensing of third-party dependencies under different terms.

NOTICE and third-party software

The repository's NOTICE file records the first-party copyright statement and summarizes third-party groups. Exact resolved dependency versions are pinned in gradle/libs.versions.toml and the desktop and server lockfiles.

Third-party components remain under their own licenses — predominantly Apache-2.0, MIT and BSD-3-Clause, with test-only EPL-1.0 components that are not distributed in runtime artifacts. See docs/dependency-licenses.md and dependency-inventory.md.

Historical license

Earlier commits were published under AGPL-3.0-only. The project relicensed first-party code to Apache-2.0 on 12 November 2026 (ADR 0015). Anyone who obtained a copy under the earlier license keeps the rights that copy granted; the change cannot revoke them. No release, tag or published artifact was ever made under the earlier license.

Contributions

Inbound contributions use the same license with Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off. There is no copyright assignment.
